# Short CV

I am a “Distinguished Professor” of Aerospace Engineering at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM). I am a Mechanical Engineer and hold a Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering. My research focuses on modern high-order Discontinuous Galerkin (DG) methods for computational fluid dynamics and plasma physics, as well as in developing high-performance parallel computing tools.
